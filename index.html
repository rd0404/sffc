SFFC Fantasy League — Rules for the Game, 2026-27.

A. Basic Rules
  1. There will be 20 teams consisting of 6 players each.
  2. Each team will have the name of a current Premier League team, no repeats.
  3. The team name cannot be changed once it is allocated.
  4. Out of the 6 members, 1 member will be appointed as the Team Manager.
  5. The manager communicates with the admins directly and no one else. Queries from members should go through the Manager.
  6. Direct queries from members other than the Manager will not be entertained.
  7. The assigned team manager can only be changed once in the entire season.
  8. Each team will be provided with a passcode for captain submission.
B. Fixtures
  1. The season is played in 2 halves of 19 GWs each — Phase 1: GW1–19, Phase 2: GW20–38 (GW names stay unchanged for the 2nd half).
  2. Fixtures follow the real Premier League fixture list exactly.
  3. Double and blank gameweeks don't affect SFFC-FL fixtures — the original fixture list is always followed.
C. Entry Fee
  1. Entry fee is INR 400 per member, covering the full 38 GWs — no extra fee for Phase 2.
  2. Paid at the start of the season, collected by the manager and transferred to the admins.
  3. Payable via Google Pay, Paytm, or bank transfer.
D. Team Name Allocation
  1. Allocated at a scheduled date/time, first come first basis, by token number after payment proof is shared.
  2. No entry fees accepted before the scheduled time.
E. Points System
  1. Every player registers on the official FPL site and creates a team.
  2. One of the 6 members is selected as captain each GW; the manager submits this via passcode.
  3. Captain points are doubled — choose wisely.
  4. All 6 members' scores are added and matched against the opponent's total (minus any transfer-hit negatives).
  5. Higher scoring team wins: 3 points for a win, 1 for a draw.
F. Prizes
  1. Top 3 at the end of GW19 win Phase 1 cash prizes; same for GW38 and Phase 2.
  2. 1st: Rs. 5,400 · 2nd: Rs. 4,200 · 3rd: Rs. 3,000.
  3. Highest-scoring team each GW is Team of the Week — Rs. 420 to the manager, shared with the team. Captain doubling isn't counted for this.
G. Rules for Teams
  1. All members join the Classic SFFC-FL league.
  2. The Team Manager creates an FPL classic league including all 6 members, named after their club followed by SFFC (e.g. "Chelsea (SFFC)").
  3. No member may change their FPL name/team name without informing admins — unannounced changes are penalised.
  4. Managers must share every member's details and the mini-league link once the team name is allotted.
  5. Up to 2 members can be replaced per season, from Free Agents where available.
  6. A replacement member cannot reuse a chip the old member already played.
H. Captains
  1. A captain is set for every GW from the 6 members.
  2. Each player must be captained at least twice per phase, completed by GW16, or the team is penalised.
  3. Captain submission is only valid via the form — no text messages.
  4. Deadline matches the official FPL deadline.
  5. If no captain is submitted, the lowest scorer is auto-captained (not counted toward the minimum).
  6. A submitted captain can be changed up to twice per phase by contacting admins before the deadline.
  7. Keep a screenshot of the confirmation message — it's the only valid proof in a dispute.
  8. The "Max Captain" chip (once per phase) auto-selects the highest scorer as captain; it's chosen via the Captain Submission Form and doesn't count toward the Captain Count.
  9. Disputes are only heard between the FPL deadline and 1.5 hours after (kickoff of the first match), with valid proof.
I. Penalties
  1. Missed captain-count criteria: 1st offence Yellow Card (-1), 2nd offence 2nd Yellow (-2), 3rd offence Red Card (-3).
  2. FPL name changes: Warning, then Yellow (-1), 2nd Yellow (-2), Red Card (-3) for repeat offences.
  3. Missing the minimum captain count deducts 3 points per missed count — from the league table, not that week's team score.
J. Champions League
  1. Top 8 from Phase 1 qualify, entering directly at the Quarter Finals.
  2. Seeding follows Phase 1 standings — no extra entry fee.
  3. Winner: Rs. 1,800 · Runner-up: Rs. 1,200.
K. Phase Change
  1. Phase 1 ends GW19, Phase 2 starts GW20.
  2. During the changeover, teams may make up to 12 transfers beyond their free transfers (up to -48 points, not otherwise penalised) split however the team likes.
  3. Transfers beyond that follow normal FPL transfer-hit rules.
L. Admin Rights
  1. Admins may amend the rulebook at any time during the season, and will inform team managers of any changes.
